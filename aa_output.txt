# LLM Leaderboard - Comparison of AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, SpaceXAI & others

Comparison and ranking the performance of over 250 AI models (LLMs) across key metrics including intelligence, price, performance and speed (output speed - tokens per second & latency - TTFT), context window & others.

For more details including relating to our methodology, see our [FAQs.](/faq)

[LLM API Providers Leaderboard](/leaderboards/providers)[Microevals Playground](/microevals)

#### Intelligence

Updated

Claude Fable 5.1 (max with fallback) and GPT-6 Astra (max) are the highest intelligence models, followed by GPT-6 Astra (xhigh) and Claude Opus 5 (max).

![](/img/logos/anthropic_small.svg)![](/img/logos/openai_small.svg)![](/img/logos/openai_small.svg)![](/img/logos/anthropic_small.svg)

#### Output Speed

Celeris-1 and Mercury 2 are the fastest models, followed by Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and LFM2.5-VL-1.6B.

![](/img/logos/celeris.svg)![](/img/logos/inceptionlabs_small.svg)![](/img/logos/google_small.svg)![](/img/logos/liquidai_small.svg)

#### Latency

Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite (Non-reasoning) and Command A+ are the lowest latency models, followed by North Mini Code and Gemini 2.5 Flash (Non-reasoning).

![](/img/logos/google_small.svg)![](/img/logos/cohere_small.svg)![](/img/logos/cohere_small.svg)![](/img/logos/google_small.svg)

#### Cost per Task

Granite 4.2 3B and Granite 4.2 8B have the lowest cost per task, followed by Ling 3.0 Flash and MiMo-V2.5-Pro.

![](/img/logos/ibm_small.svg)![](/img/logos/ibm_small.svg)![](/_next/image?url=%2Fimg%2Flogos%2Finclusionai_small.jpg&w=32&q=75)![](/img/logos/xiaomi_small.svg)

#### Context Window

Llama 4 Scout and Grok 4.20 0309 support the largest context windows, followed by Gemini 1.5 Pro (May) and Grok 4.1 Fast.

![](/img/logos/meta_small.svg)![](/img/logos/spacexai.svg)![](/img/logos/google_small.svg)![](/img/logos/spacexai.svg)
