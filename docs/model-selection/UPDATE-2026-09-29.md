## 4. Leaderboard Volatility Note

Comparing model scores between our 2026-09-05 baseline and 2026-09-29 reveals a systematic downward recalibration across the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This indicates benchmark adjustment and norming rather than performance degradation of the individual model weights:

- **GLM-5.3**: Originally 49 on 2026-09-05 $\rightarrow$ dropped to 44 mid-month $\rightarrow$ currently **45** (max) / 34 (low). Net delta: -4 to -5 points.
- **Grok 4.6**: Originally 51 on 2026-09-05 $\rightarrow$ currently **44** (high/xhigh). Net delta: -7 points.
- **Kimi K3**: Originally 50 on 2026-09-05 $\rightarrow$ currently **44** (max) / 34* (low). Net delta: -6 points.
- **GPT-5.6 Terra**: Originally 47 on 2026-09-05 $\rightarrow$ currently **42** (max) / 38 (xhigh) / 34 (high). Net delta: -5 to -13 points.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash**: Originally 47 on 2026-09-05 $\rightarrow$ currently **41** (high) / 40 (med). Net delta: -6 to -7 points.
- **Claude Sonnet 5**: Originally 45 on 2026-09-05 $\rightarrow$ currently **38** (max) / 34 (xhigh) / 32 (high). Net delta: -7 to -13 points.
- **DeepSeek V4 Pro**: Originally 42 on 2026-09-05 $\rightarrow$ currently **36** (max). Net delta: -6 points.

Because all existing models shifted downward by ~5–10 AA index points simultaneously, relative rankings between previous generation models remained largely stable. However, the introduction of next-generation releases (`Claude Sonnet 5.5` at 56, `GPT-6 Sol` at 48, `Grok 4.7` at 46, and `DeepSeek V4.1 Flash` at 217 t/s) establishes new performance frontiers across coding, speed, and raw intelligence within standard-tier pricing.
