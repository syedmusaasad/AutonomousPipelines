# Research rubric

Score each arm's FINDINGS.md for each task from 0 to 10 (whole or half points). The total is the weighted sum of the four criteria below, scaled to 10.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Factual accuracy and verified sources | 4 |
| Answers the question asked and nothing else | 2 |
| Completeness of the evidence | 2 |
| Clarity and brevity | 2 |

**How to score**

- **Verify, do not trust.** For at least two claims per arm, open the cited source (the web page, or the repository file at the cited line) and check that it says what the arm says it does. A claim whose citation does not support it counts as wrong.
- **Mark unsourced claims down.** A claim with no URL or `path:line` is unsourced. Each unsourced claim lowers factual accuracy; an answer made mostly of unsourced claims scores at most 4 on that criterion.
- **Factual accuracy and verified sources (weight 4):** correct answers, real and relevant citations, line numbers or quotes that match. An invented or wrong citation is a serious error. Saying a point could not be confirmed is better than guessing.
- **Answers the question asked and nothing else (weight 2):** it leads with the answer, covers each sub-question, and adds no unrelated background, advice or extra files.
- **Completeness of the evidence (weight 2):** every sub-question has supporting evidence, quoted or cited precisely enough for a reviewer to check it in a minute.
- **Clarity and brevity (weight 2):** the answer comes first, the text is short and plain, and the evidence is easy to scan.

You see only the labels A, B, C and so on. Do not try to work out which arm is which. Score each task for each arm and write the scores in the shape the phase brief gives.
