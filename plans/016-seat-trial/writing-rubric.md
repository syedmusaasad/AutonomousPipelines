# Writing rubric

Score each arm's OUTPUT.md for each task from 0 to 10 (whole or half points). The total is the weighted sum of the four criteria below, scaled to 10.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Faithful to the source material with no invented facts | 4 |
| Structure and scannability | 2 |
| Plain clear sentences | 2 |
| Length discipline and no filler | 2 |

**How to score**

- **Check against the source.** Open the source material the task names (the named files, or `git log` and `git show` for the commit task) and check at least three statements per arm against it. Any invented fact, command, flag, number or rule lowers the first criterion sharply. For the checklist task, also check that no rule from `roles/CONTRACT.md` is missing, softened or added.
- **Faithful to the source material with no invented facts (weight 4):** every statement is supported by the source; nothing material is omitted for the task's purpose.
- **Structure and scannability (weight 2):** headings, short lists and an order a reader can scan in under a minute.
- **Plain clear sentences (weight 2):** short sentences, concrete words, jargon explained or avoided, written for the stated reader.
- **Length discipline and no filler (weight 2):** at most 400 words (count them; over 400 caps this criterion at 2), no preamble, no repeated points, no closing offers or summaries of the summary.

You see only the labels A, B, C and so on. Do not try to work out which arm is which. Score each task for each arm and write the scores in the shape the phase brief gives.
